Put your site images in this folder:

  header.png      the picture at the top of every page
  promo.png       the picture on the home page
  rules.png       optional picture on the rules page
  preview.png     shown when a link to your site is shared on Discord
                   or social media (recommend at least 1200x630px)
  background.png  only if you choose an image background

Then check the file names in src/config.mjs match.
